Granular Ball Computing

Granular Ball Computing (GBC) is an adaptive multi-granularity artificial intelligence learning paradigm proposed by Guoyin Wang and Shuyin Xia. It replaces traditional fine-grained point-based inputs with differently sized hyperspheres (granular balls) as mesoscopic representation units, aiming to simultaneously improve the efficiency, robustness, and interpretability of existing AI methods.

G-GBC: A Gaussian Mixture-Based Granular Ball Computing Method for Robust CNN Classification under Noisy Labels

Junxiao Yang, Qiang Liu, Xuemei Cao, Fei Li, Xin Yang

Knowledge-Based Systems, 2026

In actual scenarios, both manual and automated annotation methods are susceptible to generating mislabeled data. Existing noise-handling techniques primarily focus on penalizing or eliminating noisy labels; however, they often face challenges in maintaining interpretability and effectively addressing the complexities of label noise. To address these issues, we propose a novel framework that leverages the robustness and interpretability of granular-ball computing (GBC) to improve noise resistance and classification performance.
